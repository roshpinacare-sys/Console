Ad pack v2: say less, prove more

v2, 2026-09-28. Replaces the 2026-09-12 pack, audited against disclosure policy v2: no internal figures, no mechanism details, no machine-sounding lines. Source of truth: Adsmarket/content/ads/ads-pack-v2.md.
Every unit here is ready to copy. Each one carries a visual direction. Iron rules: zero emojis · zero promises of returns · zero internal figures · one claim per unit · a "come see" CTA, never a "buy" CTA · when a number is needed, it lives behind a link, inside a public receipt.

A. Facebook / Instagram

A1: "The wall" (video ad)

Visual: A long wall in low light. Paper receipts appear one by one, each stamped with a time. The wall never stops growing. Text: Everybody talks. Some of it is even true. The part that matters is the part you can check. We keep a wall of receipts in a public place. Come read it. Button: "See the wall"

A2: "The quiet replacement" (comparison, no numbers)

Visual: An old balance scale. One side: an ink stamp and a stack of forms. The other side: one thin receipt. The thin side sinks. Text: Proving something existed used to take a stamp, a form and a line. Now it takes a fingerprint in a public ledger, and anyone can read it back. We will not quote the price here. Come look. It will spoil your assumptions. Button: "Check it yourself"

A3: "We were wrong once" (trust builder)

Visual: A ledger book, open. One line struck through. The corrected line sits next to it. Nothing else on the page. Text: Once we published a status that turned out to be wrong. The mistake is still up, with the correction next to it. A system that can afford to show its mistakes is a system worth checking. Button: "Read the journal"

G. Tone law

  1. Zero em-dashes in published copy. Periods, commas, full stops.
  2. If a line sounds like a deck or a bot, cut it.
  3. Short, long, short. No parallel triads. No "it's not just X, it's Y".
  4. Say less than you know. Point at the receipt instead of unloading it.
  5. Zero internal money. Zero cadences, counters, component counts, failure causes.
  6. Hebrew reads like speech. If it reads translated, it gets cut.
  7. One claim per unit. CTA is always "come see", never "buy".

H. Visual directions v2

  1. Palette: a near-black background (#09090b), white text, a gold accent (#d4af37). The feel of "an official document in the dark", not of a casino.
  2. Numbers: monospace only, and only when the figure comes from a public receipt behind a link. No inline figures anywhere.
  3. Forbidden: rockets, rising green graphs, people in sunglasses, "x100", countdowns, emojis, dashboards with balances, labeled server racks, price tags with exact figures, infrastructure diagrams of any kind.
  4. Encouraged: journals, wax seals, paper receipts, scales, ledgers, a wall of paper, one blinking light in a dark room.
  5. Every ad = one claim. Never mix notary + bridge + trading in the same unit.
