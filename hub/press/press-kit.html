Hard questions: our ready answers

"Do you promise returns?" No, and we never have. Anyone promising returns in our name is impersonating us. "Have you had an external audit?" Not yet. The audit package (code byte-identical to production + a MANIFEST) is packaged and waiting; we will publish the findings when they arrive, including if they are harsh. "What happens if you disappear tomorrow?" Everything already recorded stays recorded in the public ledgers. The proofs do not depend on our existence. "Why not just use Chainlink?" Because they ask for trust in a committee plus gas; we publish data anyone can read on their own, keyless and free. The difference is philosophy, not price. "Your numbers sound too good" True, which is why every one of them is backed by links. If a single link fails, our whole building falls, and that is exactly the idea. "How do you protect the keys?" Defense-in-depth as architecture, not as a wall: keys never touch the repo, the code, the files, or the git history (verified scans on every push, history checked clean) · THE-GATE: login verified against the chain itself (paste → on-chain verification → vault-first) · an encrypted multi-home vault, three homes, mirrored, self-healing, 144/144 recovered (v31) · fund release at a 7/11 threshold (EIP-712) · fail-closed authorization gates on 10 sensitive operations, 9/9 red vectors blocked live (RESHUT) · an anti-phishing gate on every outbound link (MAGEN) · an isolated external sealer for broadcasts. Zero secrets in any push, ever. Internal policy: security incidents are handled and documented internally; externally, the architecture is what we show. "Has your system ever crashed?" Yes. On 2026-09-08 23:58 the kernel killed the web layer (OOM, 1.79GB/4GB, proven in dmesg). The keeper we added revived it within ~36 seconds, without a human touch, with a full trail in an append-only journal. There is also mutual watchdogging (the runner revives the keeper, proven live) and a zombie detector ("a live process is not truth, block progress is the truth"). The full incident story is shown in the "Keeper" view in the terminal, including the incident itself. "Is there a product one can buy/install today?" The product is live and proven end to end inside the system: an Identity Verification API + a zero-dependency SDK, metered with a daily quota and a full ledger. For full external availability, two owner actions remain in progress: publishing the package to npm and opening a public gateway. We draw a loud line between internal and external availability; that is our disclosure policy.
