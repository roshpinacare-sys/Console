Production directions

  1. Sound: quiet. Minimal piano or complete silence with paper/stamp sounds. Nothing aggressively electronic.
  2. Narration: a calm voice, speaking at eye level, like explaining to a friend. Not an ad announcer.
  3. On-screen text: always the exact number (32,603 · $0.0000081 · 383,836) in monospace. The numbers are the stars.
  4. Forbidden: countdowns, "last chance", tension music, stock-exchange stock footage with green charts.
  5. Subtitles: always, most viewing happens without sound. Hebrew, large and readable.
  6. Versions: for every script, produce an English version as well (a direct translation from the fact-sheet, not machine translation).
