Questions people ask

How do I open a SAOS wallet?

Press Open a free wallet, then Create. Your key is generated on your device and your address is derived from it. Nothing is sent to a server and no account is needed.

Who holds my keys?

Only you. Keys are generated in your browser and stay in memory. You can save an encrypted backup with a password you choose. There is no recovery and no custody.

How can I check the numbers myself?

The whole ledger is public. Press Verify in the Network page and your browser reads every operation from the public chain, checks every signature and compares the result with what this site shows.

What do deposits do?

Deposits fund network development and are converted to SAOS at the current network rate. Every deposit, credit and reward is recorded in the open ledger. No promise of profit is made or implied.

How it is built

1
Your address comes from your key

An address is a fingerprint of the public key alone. Whoever holds the key owns the address. No registry, no approval.

2
The contract is code that everyone runs

Supply rules, escrow and balances are enforced by one deterministic engine that runs identically in every browser and node.

3
Operations are signed, transport cannot forge

Every operation carries the owner's signature. Public relayers move bytes; they cannot change them.

4
The cap is a law of the engine

21,000,000 SAOS maximum, checked at every mint by every node. Breaking it would need every verifier to agree to lie.
